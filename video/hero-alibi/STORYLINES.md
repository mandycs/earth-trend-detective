## A. Three places, one question (picked)
- Device: the result first, three times. Form: cut on the beat between three time-lapses.
- Seen: a reservoir draining year by year, a glacier pulling back, a wetland's lagoons, each as real
  aerial imagery flicking through the years under a bronze, high-contrast grade; then the question
  "Is it the climate?" and Lake Mead's answer as a range.
- First 2 s: Lake Mead in 2003, full of water, the year "2003" slammed on screen on the first drum hit.
- Beats: Lake Mead drains → Aletsch retreats → Doñana floods and dries → "Is it the climate?" →
  "At Lake Mead, 54–84% is not. A clue, not proof." → Earth Trend Detective.
- Look: crushed blacks, bronze highlights, 2.39:1 letterbox, League Gothic caps in bone.
- Sound: war-drum percussion with low strings, hits on each case cut, one riser into the question.

## B. One continuous dive
- Form: one take from the globe down into Lake Mead, then out and across to the Alps and Doñana.
- Left behind: needs a baked globe flight (network tiles) and reads as a map demo more than a story.

## C. The bathtub ring
- Device: one number made physical. Lake Mead only, the waterline dropping 29 m in 3D with a level gauge.
- Left behind: strongest single image, but drops the "it is not always us" beat that Aletsch carries.
